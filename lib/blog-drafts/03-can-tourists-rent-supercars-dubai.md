## The Documents You Actually Need

The requirements split cleanly by where you're from.

### If You're From the US, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, or Most of the EU

**You can drive on your home licence alone.** No International Driving Permit needed. The UAE's Road and Transport Authority (RTA) has a reciprocal agreement with approximately 40 countries whose driving standards are considered equivalent.

What to bring to handover:
- Your **original passport** (with UAE entry stamp or tourist visa)
- Your **home driving licence** (physical card, not a photo or digital copy)
- A **credit card** in your name (for the security deposit)

### If You're From India, China, Russia, Brazil, South Africa, or Most of Asia/Africa/South America

You need an **International Driving Permit (IDP)** alongside your home licence.

Critical rule: **You must get the IDP in your home country before you fly.** IDPs cannot be issued in the UAE. There is no workaround at the rental desk.

Where to get an IDP:
- **UK:** Post Office walk-in, ~10 minutes, £5.50
- **US:** AAA branches, ~$20
- **India:** Local RTO, ~₹1,000
- **Australia:** RAC/NRMA/RACV clubs, ~AUD 50
- **Most other countries:** National automobile association

### What Doesn't Work

- Digital copies of your licence (must be physical)
- Expired licences (must be valid for the rental duration)
- Debit cards for deposits (credit card required)
- Cash deposits at handover (legitimate companies won't accept this)
- Driving without a valid licence/IDP (AED 5,000+ fine + vehicle impoundment)

## Security Deposits for Tourists

This is the part that causes the most anxiety — and the most Reddit threads.

**How it works:** The rental company places a pre-authorisation hold on your credit card. This is not a charge — the money stays in your account but is unavailable to spend.

**How much:** AED 5,000–15,000 depending on the car. A [Porsche 911](/rent-porsche-dubai/porsche-911-carerra-s-spyder) requires around AED 5,000. A [Lamborghini Huracan](/rent-lamborghini-dubai/lamborghini-huracan-evo-coupe) is AED 5,000–10,000.

**When it's released:** Within 15 working days after return, once Salik tolls and any RTA fines have cleared.

**Single most common tourist mistake:** Not having enough available credit on their card. If your credit limit is AED 10,000 and the rental plus deposit totals AED 14,000, you'll be turned away.

**Pro tip:** Some cars qualify for no-deposit options. Ask the concierge before booking.
