## The Real Cost: What Adds Up

Here's where first-time renters get surprised — because no one tells you about these four things.

### 1. Security Deposits

Every supercar rental in Dubai requires a refundable security deposit held as a pre-authorisation on your credit card.

| Model | Typical Deposit (AED) |
|---|---|
| Porsche 911, Mercedes G63 | 5,000 |
| Lamborghini Huracan, Ferrari F8 | 5,000–10,000 |
| Rolls-Royce, McLaren 765LT | 10,000 |
| Lamborghini Aventador / Revuelto | 15,000+ |

**Important:** This is a hold, not a charge. The funds stay in your account but aren't available to spend. It's released within 15 working days after return — once Salik tolls and any traffic fines have cleared the RTA system.

### 2. Insurance: CDW vs Comprehensive

Basic third-party insurance is included by law. But the deductible (excess) can be AED 5,000–20,000 per incident — meaning a minor scrape costs you thousands.

**Collision Damage Waiver (CDW):** Included in the rate. Reduces your excess significantly but doesn't eliminate it.

**Zero-Excess Coverage:** Available as an add-on for most cars (AED 100–500/day). Reduces your liability to zero. Worth it for first-time supercar drivers unfamiliar with Dubai's road conditions.

**What's typically excluded:** Tyres, windscreens, underbody damage, off-road driving, and driving under the influence. Read the [booking terms](/booking-tcs) carefully.

### 3. Salik Tolls

Dubai's electronic toll system charges AED 4 per gate crossing. A typical day of driving — hotel to Dubai Mall, to JBR, to Palm Jumeirah, back to hotel — hits 4–8 gates.

**Daily Salik cost:** AED 16–32  
**On a 3-day rental:** AED 48–96

Every rental car comes with a Salik tag fitted. Tolls are tracked and billed at cost after return.

### 4. Mileage Caps

Most supercar rentals in Dubai come with a **250 km per day** mileage limit. This is generous enough for city cruising but worth tracking if you're planning day trips:

| Route | Approx. Distance | Over cap? |
|---|---|---|
| Dubai Marina → Downtown → JBR (day out) | 40–60 km | No |
| Dubai → Abu Dhabi return | 280 km | Yes (~30 km over) |
| Dubai → Jebel Jais (RAK) return | 220 km | No |
| Dubai → Hatta return | 260 km | Yes (~10 km over) |

**Overage fees:** Typically AED 15–30 per excess kilometre. Ask about **unlimited mileage upgrades** (usually AED 200–600/day extra) if you're planning long road trips.

## When to Rent for the Best Price

**November to March:** Peak tourist season. Prices are at their highest. Book 2–3 weeks ahead.

**June to August:** Summer low season. Prices drop 20–30%. The car is air-conditioned. The roads are quieter. The only downside is stepping out into 45°C heat.

**Weekdays vs Weekends:** Thursday and Friday (UAE weekend) are the most expensive. Monday to Wednesday offer better rates.

**Longer rentals:** Weekly rates work out to roughly 6× the daily rate. Monthly works out to roughly 26×. Ask on WhatsApp for the exact package.

## The Bottom Line

Dubai remains the best-value market in the world for supercar rentals. A Lamborghini Huracan at AED 4,000/day (∼$1,090 USD) compares to $1,800+/day in Miami or Monaco. The roads are better, the infrastructure is built for cars, and the experience of driving past the Burj Khalifa at sunset is genuinely hard to replicate anywhere else.

**Budget an extra AED 500–1,000 per day** beyond the headline rate for insurance, tolls, and fuel. Bring a credit card with AED 10,000–15,000 of available headroom for the deposit hold. Drive within the speed limits. And don't take the Ferrari into the desert.
