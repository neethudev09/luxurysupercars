# What Is the Best Supercar to Rent for a Weekend in Dubai?

*First published: July 2026 · Reading time: 6 minutes*

---

A weekend in Dubai is the perfect amount of time for a supercar rental. Long enough to plan a proper route — sunset on Sheikh Zayed Road, dinner in DIFC, a morning cruise to the Palm — short enough that the cost feels worth every dirham.

But which car do you pick?

The answer depends on what kind of weekend you're planning. Here are my picks for every vibe, budget, and itinerary.

## The "I Want the Full Dubai Experience" Weekend

**Pick: [Lamborghini Huracan EVO Spyder](/rent-lamborghini-dubai/lamborghini-huracan-evo-spyder-yellow)**

This is the most-rented supercar in Dubai for a reason. The Huracan EVO Spyder delivers the complete package: the V10 naturally aspirated engine note, the convertible top for evening drives, and the unmistakable Lamborghini silhouette that photographs perfectly against the Burj Khalifa.

**Weekend itinerary:**
- **Friday evening:** Collect the car (delivered to your hotel). Drive [Sheikh Zayed Road](/locations/business-bay) toward Downtown as the sun sets. Park at [City Walk](/locations/city-walk) for dinner.
- **Saturday morning:** Top-down cruise to [Palm Jumeirah](/locations/palm-jumeirah). Brunch at one of the beach clubs. Photograph the car against the Atlantis skyline.
- **Saturday night:** Dinner at Nobu Dubai. The valet line at Atlantis The Royal is where a Huracan earns its keep.
- **Sunday morning:** One last drive along [JBR](/locations/jbr) before return.

**Rate:** AED 4,000/day  
**Deposit:** AED 5,000–10,000  
**Min. age:** 25

## The "I Want Performance Without the Supercar Price" Weekend

**Pick: [Porsche 911 Turbo S](/rent-porsche-dubai/porsche-911-turbo-s)**

The Turbo S is the smartest supercar rental in Dubai. It's genuinely fast (0–100 in 2.6 seconds — faster than most Ferraris), all-wheel drive for confidence on Dubai's highways, and comfortable enough that you won't mind being in it for hours.

What makes it the best weekend value: you get 90% of the supercar experience at 50% of the Lamborghini/Ferrari price point.

**Weekend itinerary:**
- **Saturday:** Early morning drive to [Jebel Jais](/locations/ras-al-khaimah) in Ras Al Khaimah. The 22 km mountain road is the best driving route in the UAE, and the Porsche's AWD and dual-clutch gearbox make it the perfect car for the climb. Allow 4 hours round trip from Dubai Marina.
- **Sunday:** Lazy afternoon in [Dubai Marina](/locations/dubai-marina). Lunch on the water. Evening drinks at a rooftop bar.

**Rate:** AED 3,000/day  
**Deposit:** AED 5,000  
**Min. age:** 21

## The "I Want Absolute Luxury" Weekend

**Pick: [Rolls-Royce Cullinan](/rent-rolls-royce-dubai/rolls-royce-cullinan)**

Not every Dubai weekend needs to be about speed. Sometimes you want to arrive. The Cullinan is the most comfortable way to move around Dubai — whisper-quiet cabin, commanding driving position, and the kind of presence that makes everything feel like a VIP arrival.

**Weekend itinerary:**
- **Friday:** Afternoon tea at Burj Al Arab. The Cullinan is the only car that looks appropriately regal in that valet line.
- **Saturday:** Visit the new museums and galleries in [DIFC](/locations/difc). Dinner at Zuma.
- **Sunday:** Shopping at Dubai Mall. The Cullinan's boot fits everything.

**Rate:** AED 5,500/day  
**Deposit:** AED 10,000  
**Min. age:** 25

## The "I Want Instagram Gold" Weekend

**Pick: [Ferrari 296 GTS Spyder](/rent-ferrari-dubai/ferrari-296-gts-spyder)**

The 296 GTS is Ferrari's latest hybrid convertible — and it photographs better than any car in the fleet. The clean, sculptural lines. The V6 hybrid powertrain that sounds surprisingly good. The roof that drops in 14 seconds.

**Weekend itinerary:**
- **Friday afternoon:** Sunset photoshoot on [Palm Jumeirah](/locations/palm-jumeirah) crescent. The light at golden hour + the Ferrari silhouette = your most-liked Instagram post.
- **Saturday:** Day trip to [Abu Dhabi](/locations/abu-dhabi). The E11 highway is the Ferrari's natural habitat — comfortable at speed, effortless to cruise.
- **Sunday:** Brunch at [Bluewaters Island](/locations/bluewaters-island) with the Ain Dubai view.

**Rate:** AED 4,000/day  
**Deposit:** AED 7,500  
**Min. age:** 25

## The "I'm Bringing Friends" Weekend

**Pick: [Mercedes-AMG G63](/rent-mercedes-benz-dubai/mercedes-benz-amg-g63-800-widestar)**

The G-Wagen is Dubai's unofficial national car. It fits four adults in genuine comfort, has the road presence of a supercar, and works for everything from desert-edge road trips to valet parking at the city's best restaurants.

**Weekend itinerary:**
- **Friday:** Family/friend dinner at one of the Palm restaurants. The G63 fits everyone.
- **Saturday:** Day trip to [Al Qudra](/locations/motor-city) lakes. The G63's 4WD capability means you can explore the dirt tracks around the lakes safely — just keep it on designated roads.
- **Sunday:** Brunch at Nobu. Valet park. The G63 looks as appropriate at a supercar valet line as it does at a shopping centre.

**Rate:** AED 3,000/day  
**Deposit:** AED 5,000  
**Min. age:** 21

## The "Budget Is No Object" Weekend

**Pick: [Lamborghini Revuelto](/rent-lamborghini-dubai/lamborghini-revuelto)**

The Revuelto is Lamborghini's flagship hybrid V12 — 1,001 horsepower, three electric motors, and the last V12 Lamborghini that will ever exist without full electrification. There are very few Revueltos available for rent in Dubai, making this a genuinely rare experience.

**Weekend itinerary:**
- Fly in Friday morning. Car delivered to DXB Arrivals. Drive directly to [Atlantis The Royal](/locations/palm-jumeirah).
- Spend the weekend doing the best Dubai route: Sheikh Zayed Road → Palm Jumeirah → JBR → Bluewaters → DIFC → Downtown.
- Return Sunday evening. You won't forget this weekend.

**Rate:** AED 11,000/day  
**Deposit:** AED 15,000+  
**Min. age:** 25
